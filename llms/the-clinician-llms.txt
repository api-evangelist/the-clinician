# The Clinician

> The Clinician is a New Zealand-headquartered digital health company whose product, The Clinician
> Platform (TCP, formerly ZEDOC), is an outcomes-intelligence layer for value-based healthcare. It
> captures patient-reported outcome and experience measures (PROMs, PREMs, CROMs) across SMS, email,
> web and native mobile in 20+ languages, scores and benchmarks them automatically against MCID
> thresholds, runs configurable digital care pathways with a threshold-triggered alert engine, and
> returns outcomes to the EMR via FHIR. It sits alongside an EMR/PAS rather than replacing it.

## What the platform does

- Patient-reported measures capture: validated instruments (EQ-5D, KOOS, OHS, PROMIS and hundreds
  more) delivered omnichannel at low patient burden, in 20+ languages including right-to-left scripts.
- Digital care pathways: a library of ~60 ICHOM-aligned pathways (hip and knee, heart failure,
  Parkinson's, maternity, depression and anxiety, diabetes, dialysis, surgery waitlist, referral
  triage) plus co-designed pathways, built with a no-code Form Builder and Pathway Editor.
- Alerting: configurable thresholds on patient-reported values with priority routing, assignee
  management and predefined action types.
- Analytics: individual and population-level analytics, MCID benchmarking, longitudinal outcome
  trajectories, cohort and service benchmarking.
- Scale claimed: 8.2m patients, 2,600 clinicians, 350 projects, five continents. A state-wide South
  Australia deployment scaling toward 5,000 clinicians and 1M+ patients.
